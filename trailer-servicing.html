Questions

GOOD TO KNOW

What size trailers do you service?

Single and tandem axle box, car and general-purpose trailers around the South West. If you're not sure whether yours is covered, just give us a call.

Do I need to be there while you service my trailer?

Not necessarily — as long as we can access it safely. Many customers leave it parked at home or work and we handle the rest.

What's included in the Trailer Safety Check?

Tyres and wheels, wheel bearings, coupling and hitch, safety chains, suspension, jockey wheel, and general chassis condition — a quick, honest read on whether your trailer's fit to tow.

What if you find something that needs fixing?

You'll get a clear explanation of what's wrong and a quote for the fix — nothing extra happens without your approval first.
