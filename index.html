Questions

GOOD TO KNOW

Will using a mobile mechanic void my new car's warranty?

No. Under Australian Consumer Law, you're entitled to choose your own repairer. We use manufacturer-spec parts and stamp your logbook, so your warranty stays fully protected.

Do you charge extra to travel to my area?

A small call-out fee applies depending on your location, covering travel and on-site diagnostic time. It's always confirmed upfront in your quote — no surprises on the day.

How can I pay?

Cash, bank transfer, or PayID — whatever's easiest for you.

What happens if it's raining on the day of my service?

Light rain usually isn't a problem. For more severe weather, we'll get in touch to reschedule at no cost to you — safety first, for your car and for us.
